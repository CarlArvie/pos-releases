# Detailed Device Installation & Sideloading Guide

### Minimum System Requirements
* **Operating System:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher (Android 8.1, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15)
* **RAM:** 2 GB or more
* **Storage:** At least 100 MB free space
* **Resolution:** 720p or higher recommended

---

### Step-by-Step Installation

#### 1. Download the APK
* Open Google Chrome or the default Android web browser on your tablet/phone.
* Navigate to: `https://github.com/CarlArvie/pos-releases/releases/latest`
* Tap on `app-release.apk` under **Assets**.
* Chrome will display a warning: *"File might be harmful. Do you want to download app-release.apk anyway?"* Tap **Download anyway**.

#### 2. Open and Authorize Installation
* Tap the downloaded file notification or open your device's **Downloads** folder and tap `app-release.apk`.
* If Android displays: *"Install unknown apps"*:
  1. Tap **Settings**.
  2. Toggle **Allow from this source** to ON.
  3. Press the back button to return to the installer.

#### 3. Complete Setup
* Tap **Install**.
* Once finished, tap **Open**.
* Sign in or enter your cashier/admin PIN code.
